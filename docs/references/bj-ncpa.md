# bj-ncpa (国家大剧院 / National Centre for the Performing Arts) — reference record

Tier 8 (value 256). Rework of the hemisphere-scale factory, which made the shell
nearly a half-sphere in the short view (real: distinctly squashed) and put the
glass as a one-side wedge (real: a lune crossing the crown along the short axis).
Real building: Paul Andreu, west of Tiananmen, completed 2007.

## Verified dimensions

| Feature | Real | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Long axis (E-W) | 212.2 m | Wikipedia / project literature |
| Short axis (N-S) | 143.6 m | same |
| Height above water | 46.3 m (≈0.64 × short semi-axis — squashed, not hemispheric) | same |
| Shell | ~18,000 titanium plates, horizontal panel seams | Wikipedia; detail photo |
| Glass | lune of 1,200+ ultra-white glass panes, meridian boundaries through the apex, "widens from top to bottom" | Wikipedia; photos below |
| Lake | ~35,500 m² artificial lake; 80 m underwater entrance corridor on the north side | Wikipedia |

## Palette (from photos, softened to pack style)

- titanium: light silver, faintly warm
- seams/mullions: darker grey-silver
- glass: dark blue-grey, darker than titanium
- water: muted teal
- entrance corridor: pale stone

## Model mapping (shell ≈0.57 high — far under the 2.65 cap; plot limits width)

- Half-ellipsoid, semi-axes x=0.68 (long) × z=0.47 (short) × y=0.55. Long/short
  ratio 1.45 ≈ the real 1.478; height deliberately fattened from the real 0.64
  squash to 1.17 of B (user direction: the egg must hold its own between the
  tier-7 祈年殿 and tier-9 央视, both >1 tall). The wide footprint and lake keep
  the "low wide egg" read in the front view and plan; the end profile is now
  near-hemispherical — recorded as deliberate stylization, not geometry error.
- Glass lune: two sphere-geometry patches (φ centred on ±z, half-angle
  asin(0.56)) scaled a hair proud; meridian boundaries pass through the apex.
- Seams: beam rings sampling the ellipsoid parallels, proud enough to cross
  the glass as the continuous mullion rhythm; sparse meridian fins on the glass.
- Water slab with the shell slightly submerged; low stone entrance box running
  north from the shell base to the lake edge (the underwater corridor).

## Uncertainties

- Exact panel-seam count (~30 rows) grouped to ~8 readable rings.
- Glass fin density simplified.
- End elevation inferred from the shared ellipsoid, not photo-verified.
