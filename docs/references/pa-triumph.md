# Arc de Triomphe reference record

## Model constraints

| Item       | Constraint used by the model                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Axes       | `x` is the broad ceremonial facade, `z` is the narrower side facade, and `y` is vertical.                                                                                                             |
| Proportion | Published dimensions are approximately 49.5 m high, 44.8 m wide, and 22.2 m deep. The CityMaker model keeps width and depth independent, with slight opening exaggeration for board-scale legibility. |
| Openings   | One tall, narrow main vault passes through the broad facades. Smaller transverse arches pass through the two main piers and are visibly lower.                                                        |
| Outline    | Four massive corner piers support a full-width upper wall, continuous sculptural frieze, projecting cornices, and a rectangular attic. The roofline remains horizontal.                               |
| Facade     | Four large sculptural groups sit low on the broad facades; rectangular battle reliefs, the continuous frieze, attic medallions, and layered cornices are simplified into chunky low-poly reliefs.     |
| Roof       | The terrace is represented as a shallow, flat stepped cap. Exact rooftop service geometry is not reproduced.                                                                                          |

## Remaining inference

- The accessible official plan is a small historical design illustration, not a modern measured roof plan. Interior wall thickness, stair/service volumes, rooftop rail details, and exact pier profiles are simplified.
- Sculptures and inscriptions are represented as abstract relief masses. Their placement and visual weight are reference-driven, but they are not literal replicas.
