## Silhouette established

- **Axes.** Length runs east–west (486 m); depth is the short north–south axis
  (154 m). The ceremonial axis runs north–south through the centre, with the
  plaza to the south and Lianhua Mountain to the north.
- **Roof profile (the identifying feature).** One long shallow **wave**, not a
  tent: a broad convex crown over the centre, sweeping down in an S-curve to a
  **low point at roughly 70 % out** where it nearly grazes the wing roofs, then
  **rising again into upturned wingtips**. Measured off ref #1 with the tower
  tops as 1.0: crown 0.81, shoulder minimum 0.28, wingtip 0.47, podium 0.14.
- **Roof surface is anticlastic.** Ref #3 wingtip crop: at the crown the roof
  barrels up along its spine (eaves slightly lower than the centreline); at the
  tips the north and south eaves **curl upward** while the centreline stays
  low. That twist is what makes the ends read as wings (大鹏展翅).
- **Roof plan.** Widest just inboard of the centre, tapering gently and rounding
  toward each tip. Roof depth/length ≈ 0.317.
- **Roof colour is two-tone.** Vivid sky blue top field with a broad **silver-grey
  fascia band** around the whole edge (refs #1, #3); the soffit is dark grey
  (ref #4). A narrow pale **spine strip** runs north–south over the crown on the
  ceremonial axis (ref #3).
- **Towers.** Gold **round** drum west, red **square** tower east — 天圆地方, flag
  colours. Both **pierce the roof** and stand in the open central court. Both
  tops are **cut on a slant that drops toward the central axis** (outer edge
  high). Confirmed independently in refs #1, #2 and #3; ref #3 shows the gold
  drum's tilted elliptical cut most clearly. Red is marginally the taller (15
  floors vs 12).
- **Base.** A continuous low glass podium runs the full length; light stone wing
  bars with ribbon glazing sit on it under each half of the roof. The centre is
  left open — you can see straight through under the crown between the towers
  (ref #1). Two splayed trapezoidal walls funnel outward to the south plaza
  stair (ref #3).
- **Columns.** Rows of white **fan / A-frame struts** carry the roof over the
  wings, tall inboard and shortening toward the low shoulders (refs #1, #3).

## Deliberate stylization (pack style, not uncertainty)

- **Vertical exaggeration ≈ 2.6×.** True length/height is 5.7:1, which at a
  1.50-unit length would be 0.26 tall and unreadable on the board. The model
  uses 1.50 long × 0.80 tall (≈ 1.9:1) while keeping the _internal_ height
  ratios (crown 0.79 of the tower top, shoulder 0.28, wingtip 0.45) intact.
  Tier 6 (国贸大厦) and tier 8 (地王大厦) are both slender towers, so the low
  wide canopy still reads as the distinct neighbour it should.
- Roof-top skylight slots and the fine column lattice are omitted as noise at
  board size; the fan columns are reduced to four Λ pairs per wing edge.
- Ribbon glazing reduced to two bands per wing rather than a literal grid.

## Uncertainties / inferences

- Exact tower slant angle is estimated from three oblique/elevation views, not
  from a drawing; the direction (dropping toward the axis) is well supported.
- Wing-bar height is set slightly below the measured 0.14 podium band so the
  roof can graze it at the shoulders as in ref #1; the published 22.8 m 东区
  figure appears to include taller blocks set back to the north.
- The roof crown in ref #1 sits marginally west of the ceremonial axis; the
  model centres both, an intentional simplification.
