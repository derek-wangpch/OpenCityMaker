# Rome — tiers 9 through 1

Reviewed 2026-09-06. Continues the separate St Peter’s Basilica (tier 11) and Colosseum (tier 10) records. These are original game miniatures, not measured reconstructions. Coordinates: x is frontage, y up, +z the principal entrance/view; no geographic orientation is asserted. Existing identifiers, values and registration are retained.

## Tier and shape decisions

| Tier | Model | Identity retained / deliberate simplification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | Vittoriano | Broad white terraces, shallow curved rear colonnade, paired end pavilions and quadrigas, central equestrian statue. Stair depth enlarged; columns, horses and sculpture grouped; carved relief omitted. |
| 8 | Trevi Fountain | Palace backdrop, giant order around three niches, attic crest, central figure and two sea-horse silhouettes, rock cascade and projecting pool. Sculptures abstracted; roof furnishings and carved detail omitted. |
| 7 | Castel Sant’Angelo | Large cylindrical drum over low enclosure and corner bastions, square upper block, bronze angel, entry stair and sparse belts/openings. Bridge and surrounding park omitted; bastions reduced to polygon blocks. |
| 6 | Pantheon | Circular drum, low stepped exterior dome with an actual open oculus, tall rectangular link and deep pedimented portico (eight front columns). Interior is a dark empty volume; ornamental orders/coffers omitted. |
| 5 | Forum ruins | Three surviving columns and broken entablature inspired by the Temple of Castor and Pollux, with a few stumps and one fallen shaft. Capitals simplified; no acanthus leaves or surveyed archaeological layout. |
| 4 | Aqueduct | Single tall arcade inspired by Aqua Claudia, thick spandrels and piers, covered rectangular water conduit. Five repeated bays, two simple stone bands; no water surface or masonry texture. |
| 3 | Insula | Brick apartment type: ground-floor shop arches, two sparse upper window rows and plain tiled roof. Complete typological reconstruction, not the present Ara Coeli ruin. |
| 2 | Domus | Low, inward-facing courtyard house; four inward-sloping roof planes around open compluvium and visible impluvium. One entrance; room divisions, rear peristyle and columns omitted. |
| 1 | Trastevere house | Compact ochre plaster house, terra-cotta pitched roof, dark green shutters and few windows. No balconies, tile grid, ornament or street furniture. |

Detail decreases by tier through visible architectural layers, not a strict polygon-count rule. Low-tier houses intentionally have fewer ornaments than the monuments. Front width and depth were controlled separately; sculptures and entrances are enlarged for thumbnail readability.

## Viewed evidence and limitations

### 9 — Vittoriano

Source: [Victor Emmanuel II Monument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Emmanuel_II_Monument).
Actually viewed Wikimedia photos **2014-03-29 Altare della Patria.jpg** (front/right oblique), **Piazza Venezia, Towards Altare della Patria.jpg** (near frontal) and **Vittorianomap.jpg** (schematic plan).
[Front image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/Piazza_Venezia%2C_Towards_Altare_della_Patria.jpg/960px-Piazza_Venezia%2C_Towards_Altare_della_Patria.jpg), [plan image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f4/Vittorianomap.jpg/500px-Vittorianomap.jpg).
They support the curved colonnade, paired higher ends, terraces and centered horse. No complete orthogonal side view obtained after follow-up search; rear thickness and terrace depths are inferred from oblique photo and plan. Pavilions/statues are intentionally exaggerated rather than surveyed.

### 8 — Trevi

Sources: [Turismo Roma](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/places/trevi-fountain), [photo collection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trevi_Fountain).
Viewed **Trevi Fountain - Roma.jpg** (near frontal) and **Fontana di Trevi - Rome, Italy - November 6, 2010 (6212401026).jpg** (strong right oblique across the pool).
[Front image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c7/Trevi_Fountain_-_Roma.jpg/330px-Trevi_Fountain_-_Roma.jpg).
These constrain the three niches, grouped giant columns, broad palace front, projecting rock bed and basin. ANSA terrace-photo page was blocked by a cookie/subscription screen and was not used as visual evidence. No independent roof plan or exact side elevation obtained; pool outline, palace thickness and rear are inferred. The basin is simplified to an ellipse.

### 7 — Castel Sant’Angelo

Sources: [Turismo Roma](https://www.turismoroma.it/it/luoghi/castel-santangelo), [Wikimedia photographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castel_Sant%27Angelo), [aerial source page](https://italiarinasc.cloudfree.jp/HText/CastelSantAngelo.htm).
Viewed **Castel Sant'Angelo at Night.jpg** (front), **Castel Sant'Angelo from Southeast, Rome, Italy.jpg** (oblique), and [satellite image](https://italiarinasc.cloudfree.jp/HPicture/CastelSantAngelo12.jpg).
The aerial shows the circular center inside the square enclosure with corner works, and the rectangular upper building. No true 90-degree elevation obtained; drum proportions and upper block depth follow oblique/aerial evidence. Bastion outlines and relative heights remain a game simplification; small rooms and access passages omitted.

### 6 — Pantheon

Sources: [Turismo Roma](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/places/pantheon), [photographs and plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantheon,_Rome), [aerial photograph source](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/why-do-ancient-roman-structures-like-the-pantheon-still-stand-the-self-healing-concrete-explained-11958632.html).
Viewed **Pantheon (Rome) - Right side and front.jpg**, **Pantheon (Rome) front 2.jpg**, **Dehio 1 Pantheon Floor plan.jpg**, and [aerial photograph](https://images.firstpost.com/uploads/2023/01/000_336R3YK.jpg?im=FitAndFill%3D%281200%2C675%29).
The plan provides independent depth/portico constraints; aerial confirms the low dome and stepped lower courses. Portico height is exaggerated for readability. Oculus and wall openings are real geometry; roof profile, coursing and thickness are stylized, not measurements. No complete orthogonal exterior side photo was available.

### 5 — Forum ruins

Source: [Temple of Castor and Pollux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Castor_and_Pollux).
Viewed **Dioskurernas tempel.jpg** and **Roma-tempiodioscuri01.jpg**, two oblique views showing three aligned columns with surviving entablature.
[Main image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3d/Dioskurernas_tempel.jpg/330px-Dioskurernas_tempel.jpg).
No measured top/side evidence acquired. This catalog item is a generic Forum fragment inspired by that ruin; podium, stumps and fallen shaft placement are an illustrative arrangement, not the real excavation plan. The smaller second photograph supports silhouette only, not carved detail.

### 4 — Aqueduct

Sources: [Appia Antica park](https://www.parcoappiaantica.it/itinerari/acquedotti/), [Parco degli Acquedotti photos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parco_degli_Acquedotti).
The official site's lazy images did not resolve. Actually viewed Wikimedia **Aquedottopan01pa.jpg** panorama and **Aqua Claudia.JPG** near-front image.
[Panorama](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/65/Aquedottopan01pa.jpg/960px-Aquedottopan01pa.jpg), [arcade image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f1/Aqua_Claudia.JPG/250px-Aqua_Claudia.JPG).
These show a single high arcade with solid masonry over the arches. Replaced the old two-level/open-water depiction and synchronized all three description languages. Short end and top section were not visually documented; conduit cross-section and dark end openings are simplified inference. Bay count is cropped for the plot.

### 3 — Insula

Source: [Insula dell’Ara Coeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insula_dell%27Ara_Coeli).
Viewed **Insula Ara Coeli - Rome (IT62) - 2021-08-27 - 2.jpg** and **- 1.jpg**, showing the surviving brick frontage, small rectangular upper openings and lower shop/arch spaces.
The images show a ruin, not a complete roofed building. The catalog's generic Roman apartment is represented as an illustrative intact type with three storeys; roof, unexposed side walls and regularized openings are reconstruction. No verified full side/top exists in these references. Omitted fine brick joints, irregular ruin edges and dense window grids.

### 2 — Domus

Sources: [Domus diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domus), [Smarthistory architectural explanation](https://smarthistory.org/roman-domestic-architecture-domus/).
Actually viewed [Domus romana Vector002.svg diagram](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e9/Domus_romana_Vector002.svg/500px-Domus_romana_Vector002.svg.png), which combines an illustrative cutaway/axonometric and plan with compluvium/impluvium labels. This is a schematic reconstruction, not a photograph or surveyed individual Rome house. It supports inward roof slopes and open center; the square footprint and omitted rear peristyle are deliberate compression for tier 2. The courtyard floor is raised and opening widened so the basin remains visible from the game camera. Complete exterior proportions are inferred. No claim to restore the specific Celio houses.

### 1 — Trastevere

Source: [Trastevere Commons gallery](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Trastevere).
Viewed **Roma-piazza santa maria in trastevere.jpg** (fronts around the square) and **Trastevere Roma Via Garibaldi 2013 03 b.jpg** (elevated district view). Available thumbnails were only 120 px: useful for warm plaster/green shutter palette and overall roof character, not exact window counts or precise geometry. This is a generic neighborhood house; two-storey scale, unseen side/back and simple gable are interpretive. No single address or exact reconstruction is asserted.
